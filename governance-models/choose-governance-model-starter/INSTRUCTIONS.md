# Choose a Governance Model for Trailhead Provisions

**Trailhead Provisions** grew by acquisition into four domain teams sharing a platform. They own
their pipelines and definitions, but a **GDPR audit** and a **CSRD sustainability mandate** create
obligations that cross every domain. Decide how to govern.

## Your task

In `exercise.ipynb`, decide a governance model and produce the three deliverables below.

## Requirements

- A **governance-model decision** (centralized / decentralized / federated), justified from
  Trailhead's domain autonomy AND its GDPR/CSRD exposure, naming which decisions stay central.
- A **RACI** across the four domains with exactly one accountable owner per governance function,
  plus the enforcement authority and an escalation path.
- A **Mermaid topology** showing the central core, the four domains, and the key cross-domain
  flows (consent, order→carbon→report).

## Provided files

- `exercise.ipynb` — your working notebook. `governance_toolkit.py`, `seed_data.py` — provided;
  do not edit. The notebook loads the platform for context; the deliverables are written analysis.
