The governance audit surfaces a standing `consent_conflict` between `customer.marketing_consent` (loyalty system) and `campaign_membership.mkt_consent` (marketing system). ~20% of records disagree.

**Design a consent reconciliation proposal** that answers:

1. **Which system is the system of record?** (Justify: which was collected with proper GDPR Art. 7 consent language?)
2. **What is the sync mechanism?** (Event-driven? Batch? Who triggers it?)
3. **What happens to the ~20% in disagreement RIGHT NOW?** (Default to restricted or permissive while reconciling? What does GDPR say?)
4. **How do you prevent this from recurring** when the next acquired brand joins?
