## Preview: The Architect's Brief Format

In the capstone project, you'll write a full Architect's Brief — a 1–2 page executive synthesis. Here's a 2-paragraph structural template showing what an executive synthesis looks like once you have chosen a governance model:

### Mini-brief structure (template)

> ***Paragraph 1 — Operating model & division of authority:*** *State the governance model you chose for Trailhead Provisions (`centralized`, `decentralized`, or `federated`) and explain how authority is divided between the central/platform roles (**Central Governance Lead**, **CSRD Reporting Owner**, **Platform Engineering**) and the four domain leads (**E-commerce Orders Lead**, **Customer Identity Lead**, **Inventory & Fulfillment Lead**, **Marketing & Campaigns Lead**). Explain how this model addresses both GDPR privacy obligations and CSRD carbon disclosure requirements.*
>
> ***Paragraph 2 — Key architectural tradeoff & mitigation:*** *Identify the primary operational tradeoff of your chosen model (for example, pre-publish gate latency vs. domain release velocity, or central policy enforcement vs. domain schema autonomy). Explain how your RACI enforcement gate and escalation path mitigate the risk of governance drift between audit cycles.*
