# Solution beats — Choose a Governance Model exercise (record in your voice)

- Frame the tension: domain autonomy vs platform-wide GDPR/CSRD obligations.
- Reason through the three models out loud; land on federated with a thin central core, and name
  exactly what stays central (classification, consent SoR, contract schema, metrics).
- Walk the RACI: emphasize ONE accountable owner per function, plus enforcement (publish gate) and
  escalation (council).
- Show the Mermaid topology; trace consent (loyalty→marketing) and order→carbon→report.
- Land the principle: minimum centralization that satisfies both regulators without a bottleneck.
